Your website is often the first interaction a potential customer has with your brand. I believe in a mobile-first approach, ensuring that your site is not only visually appealing but also easy to navigate on smartphones, tablets, and desktops. My process focuses on writing clean, semantic code that is both search engine friendly and easy to maintain.
